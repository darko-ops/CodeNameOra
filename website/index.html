Privacy by design

The line we don't cross

Your music stays yours

To pace you, Dromo needs one number per song: its tempo. It asks for that by recording ID and gets a number back. Your audio never leaves your phone — not a single second of it.

What Dromo learns about you never leaves at all. Which tracks move you, what you skip, how you respond when you're behind pace — that stays on the device, and you can delete it in one tap. Facts about music are shared; facts about you are not.

  • Your audio stays putTempo is looked up or computed locally — audio is never uploaded.
  • What it learns is yoursTaste and response data never leave the phone. Delete it any time.
  • No account required to runConnect a service when you want; skip it when you don't.
